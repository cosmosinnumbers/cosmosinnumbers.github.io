How we work

  • Every number has a primary source (NASA, ESA, JPL, peer-reviewed papers), cited in the video description.
  • Real images, always credited. Illustrations are labelled "Artist's concept". Use of NASA/ESA imagery does not imply endorsement by NASA or ESA.
  • Transparent about tools. Narration uses a synthetic voice and scripts are drafted with AI assistance, then checked against the public sources. We say so in every description.
  • No fake engagement. We do not buy likes, follows or comments.
