Privacy Policy — Cosmos tools

Last updated: 14 October 2026

"Cosmos tools" is a private application operated by the owner of the Cosmos in Numbers channels. It is used exclusively by that owner to publish the channel's own videos.

Data we access

With the owner's explicit OAuth consent, the tool accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API: upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and publishing schedules) and the owner's own TikTok account (TikTok Content Posting API: upload and publish the owner's videos). It does not access any other user's account or data.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared, sold or transferred. No viewer data, analytics data or personal data of third parties is collected or stored.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used under the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in the TikTok app settings (Security → Manage app permissions).

Contact

Through the channel's YouTube "About" page.
